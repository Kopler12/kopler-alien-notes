## What it does

`main.py` writes a single original line — a review of planet Earth left by
an alien tourist — to `/output/entry.txt` (151 characters, plain UTF-8 text):

> The locals stare at glowing rectangles all day, then pay strangers to
> remind them to go outside. Lovely planet. 4/5 stars — would visit again,
> briefly.

The joke lands from the alien's outside view: Earth's dominant species
spends its days hypnotised by small glowing rectangles, then hires other
humans to remind it to go outside — and still leaves a four-star review,
because the planet itself is lovely.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
